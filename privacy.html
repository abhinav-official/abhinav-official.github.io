Content Engine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Content Engine is a personal automation tool built and operated by Abhinav Jha for managing the owner's own YouTube channels (Interval and Mavi Psychology). It is not a public service and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API, the app accesses the owner's own YouTube account data: channel information, video uploads, thumbnails, captions, playlists and channel analytics. Access happens only with the owner's consent, on the owner's own Google account.

How data is used

The data is used solely to prepare, upload, publish and review the owner's own videos. The app runs locally on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens and channel data are stored only on that computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to any third party. No advertising, tracking or analytics services are used.

Retention and deletion

Stored data remains on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google account permissions page, which immediately stops all access.
